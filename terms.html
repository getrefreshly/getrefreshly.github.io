The product

Refreshly is a free browser extension that reloads tabs at intervals you choose. Every feature is free for everyone, with no limit on the number of tabs and nothing to buy.

Bought Pro in an earlier version?

Version 1.0 sold an optional Refreshly Pro for $7. Since version 1.1 every Pro feature is free for everyone. If you bought Pro and would like your money back, email refreshly.support@gmail.com from the address you used to pay.

Fair use

Use Refreshly in line with the rules of the websites you visit. Some sites limit how often pages can be loaded; you are responsible for choosing reasonable intervals.

No warranty

Refreshly is provided "as is". We work hard to keep it reliable, but we can't guarantee it will never miss a change or that alerts will always arrive.
